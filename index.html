Settings & privacy

How AI Inbox works

Your mail stays with you

The page talks to Gmail straight from this browser with your own Google sign-in. There is no server, no AI service and nothing paid. Mail is held in memory while the page is open and is never saved to this device.

Sorting

Plain rules sort the Primary inbox from the last 30 days into suggested to-dos, topics, needs reply and waiting on. Each item says why it is there.

Your marks

Mark done, Remove and Add to to-dos are Gmail labels under “AI Inbox”, so your Mac and phone agree. Archive takes a thread out of the inbox. Each change has an Undo. AI Inbox never sends and never deletes mail; draft replies wait in Gmail Drafts.

On this device

Only the sign-off above is stored in this browser. Sign out ends the Google session for this page.
