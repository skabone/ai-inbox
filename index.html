How AI Inbox works

Your mail stays with you

The page talks to Gmail straight from this browser with your own Google sign-in. There is no server, no AI service and nothing paid. Mail is held in memory while the page is open and is never saved to this device.

Sorting

Plain rules sort your inbox from the last 30 days (every tab except Promotions and Social, unless you add them above) into suggested to-dos, topics, needs reply and waiting on. Starred, pinned and to-do threads show whatever their age. Each item says why it is there.

Your marks

Mark done, Remove, Add to to-dos and Pin are Gmail labels under “AI Inbox”; Star and Important are Gmail’s own; categories are Gmail labels. So Gmail, your Mac and your phone agree. Archive takes a thread out of the inbox and Move to inbox puts it back. Each change has an Undo. AI Inbox never sends and never deletes mail or labels; draft replies wait in Gmail Drafts.

Your edits

On this device

The sign-off and the Promotions setting are stored in this browser; your edits too, only if Google Drive is not allowed. Sign out ends the Google session for this page.
